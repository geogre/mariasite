OUR STORY

Beauty that begins
with stillness.

Petal & Pore was founded on one belief: that a nail appointment should feel like a pause, not a chore. We built a studio that smells of jasmine and beeswax, sounds like ambient quiet, and feels like it was made entirely for you.

Every technician trained with us for a minimum of two years before taking a client. We use only non-toxic, cruelty-free formulas — and we never rush.

  • Non-toxic, 9-free formulas
  • Cruelty-free products only
  • Sanitised tools for every client
